# Privacy policy

Pasar Malam MTG (mtg.haziqhilmi.com)

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Pasar Malam MTG is run by one person, Muhammad Haziq bin Hilmi, in Malaysia. If you have a question about your data, or want a copy of it or want it deleted, email muhd.haziq.b.hilmi@gmail.com.

## What the app keeps

- **Your account.** When you sign in with Google: your email address, name, profile picture and Google account ID.
- **What you put in.** The photos you scan, the cards found in them, your prices and pricing settings, your posts, and the claims and orders you save.
- **Your buyers.** The names, Facebook names and phone numbers you enter for your buyers. Only you can see them.
- **Your payment details.** The bank, account number, account name, note and DuitNow QR you enter. They are added to the summaries you copy for buyers. Only you can see them, and you can delete them at any time.
- **Sales records.** When you copy or share a buyer's summary, the app records the cards in it: card name, set, finish, quantity, the final price and the date. This shows real selling prices at the night market. The record does not include the buyer's name or phone number.
- **Credits.** When you use a credit, and the vouchers you redeem.
- **Error reports and feedback.** When something breaks, or you send feedback: the message, the page, your browser type and your user number (not your email).
- **Your browser.** Your language, theme and some settings stay in your browser's local storage. There are no ads, no tracking cookies and no analytics.

Comment screenshots you add on the Claims page are read on the server to get their text. The screenshots are not kept.

## Who else gets data

- **Google Gemini** reads the comment text and the card list of a post, to find who claimed which card. Your name as the seller goes with it, so the app can tell your replies from buyers' comments.
- **Scryfall and Card Kingdom** get card names and set numbers, to find card pictures and prices. They get no personal data.
- **Telegram** delivers error reports and feedback to the developer.
- **Vercel** serves the web page, and **Cloudflare** carries the traffic to the server. The server is in Malaysia and only the developer has access to it.

Your data is not sold. Sales records can be used to work out figures across many sellers, such as the average price of a card. Any figure that is shared never shows who sold the card or who bought it.

## Your buyers' data

You enter your buyers' details, so you are responsible for having a reason to keep them, for example to arrange payment and drop-off. Delete a buyer's details when you no longer need them.

## How long data is kept

Your data stays until you delete it in the app, or until your account is deleted. To delete your account, email the address above; it is done within 30 days. When an account is deleted, its photos, posts, buyers, orders and payment details are deleted. Sales records are kept, with nothing that links them to you.

Every night the server makes a backup of the data, and it deletes each backup after 30 days. So data that you delete, or the data of a deleted account, stays in a backup for up to 30 days.

## Your rights

Under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 you can ask for a copy of your data, ask to correct it, and withdraw your consent. Email the address above.

## Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
